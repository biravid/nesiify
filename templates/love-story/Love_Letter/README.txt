Put your own photos in this folder and reference their filenames in config.js
under the "gallery" list, e.g.:

  { src: "images/memory1.jpg", caption: "That first coffee date" }

Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif

If a photo is missing or the filename doesn't match, the site will
automatically show a pretty heart placeholder instead — nothing breaks.

Recommended: square-ish photos (roughly 1:1) look best in the polaroid frames.
